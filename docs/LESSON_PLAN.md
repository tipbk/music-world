# Lesson plan

Goal: learn enough music theory, in plain words, to compose rhythm game songs and game BGM.
Each lesson = short explanation + something to play with + a small quiz.

| # | Lesson | What you learn | Interactive part | Quiz |
|---|--------|----------------|------------------|------|
| 1 | Notes & the Piano | Pitch, the 12 notes, white/black keys, octaves | Playable piano, "find the key" mini game | Which note is higher? |
| 2 | Beat & BPM | Beat, tempo, BPM, bars, rhythm game BPM ranges | Metronome, tap tempo, rhythm-game timing judge (Perfect/Great/Good/Miss) | Slow, medium or fast? |
| 3 | Rhythm & Drums | Kick/snare/clap/hi-hat, 16-step bars, how drums set energy | Step sequencer with presets | Which drum is it? |
| 4 | Scales & Keys | Scale = note palette, key = home note, major/minor/pentatonic/modes | Scale explorer on the piano | Major or minor? |
| 5 | Chords | Building triads, major/minor/dim, the 7 chords in a key, Roman numerals | Chord builder, chord pads | Major or minor chord? |
| 6 | Chord Progressions | Common progressions (pop, Royal Road, epic battle, dungeon...) and looping | Loop player with drums/style/key, build your own | Which progression is this? |
| 7 | Bass Lines | What bass does, playing the root, 5th and octave, common game bass patterns | Bass on/off demo, pattern player, bass line editor | Which bass pattern? |
| 8 | Melody | Chord tones on the beat, steps vs jumps, repetition, silence, endings | Melody grid over chords, random "safe" melody | – |
| 9 | Song Structure & Loops | Intro/verse/build-up/chorus/break/outro, BGM loops vs rhythm game songs | Section arranger with energy timeline, BGM loop mode | – |
| 10 | Game Music Recipes | Mood recipes: town, field, battle, boss, sad, victory, rhythm chorus, dungeon | Playable recipes with explanations | Which scene is it? |
| 11 | Studio | Put everything together | Full 4-bar loop editor, autosave, MIDI download | – |

Plus a searchable word list (glossary).

## Level up (advanced)

Goal: make drums, bass and melody sound alive and cool, not just "correct".

| # | Lesson | What you learn | Interactive part | Quiz |
|---|--------|----------------|------------------|------|
| 12 | Cool Drums | Accents and ghost notes, syncopation, swing, style grooves (house, breakbeat, drum & bass, half-time, lo-fi, reggaeton, rhythm game rush), fills, build-up and drop | Robot vs human switch, on/between the beat switch, swing slider, editable groove grid with loud/normal/soft hits, 4-bar fill player, 8-bar build-up → drop | Which groove? |
| 13 | Cool Melodies | Motifs and how to change them, question and answer, chord notes vs passing notes, pushed (early) notes, one tune in many moods, the high point | Motif builder (bar 1 + moves for bars 2-4), answer picker, passing-note switch, straight/pushed switch, mood machine | What happened to the motif? |
| 14 | Cool Bass Lines | Bass styles (disco octaves, off-beat, walking, funk, chiptune, pushed, half-time), teaming up with the kick, lead-in notes, arriving early | Style gallery with kick lane, follow/fill-gaps/ignore the kick, lead-in picker, on time/early switch, bass editor with Next and Lead-in rows | Which bass style? |
| 15 | Remix Lab | Combine groove, fill, bass style and motif into one loop | Recipes (rhythm game chorus, battle, cafe, boss drop) and every part editable | – |
